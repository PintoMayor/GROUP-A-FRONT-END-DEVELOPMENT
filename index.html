How it works

Three steps from complaint to completion

No forms to dig through, no office visits, no endless phone calls. Just a clear report and a public record of what happened next.

01

Describe the problem

Pick a category, tell us what is wrong and drop a pin on the exact spot. Add a photo if it helps.

02

It reaches the right office

Your report is routed to the department that owns the problem — works, sanitation, or utilities.

03

Track it to the end

Watch the status move from New to In Progress to Resolved — and see the official response.
